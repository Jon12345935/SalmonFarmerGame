# Tide & Tackle: North Atlantic

A real-time, open-world salmon-farming game in pixel-art style. Explore a large island on foot, run the farm, take side activities, and drive fish deliveries through the same world.

## Run

```powershell
python -m pip install -r requirements.txt
python salmon_farm.py
```

The first launch imports your existing `salmon_farm_save.json` career, including its cash, stock, reputation, upgrades, and loaded truck cargo. New saves are stored in `salmon_world_save.json` beside the game.

## Controls

- `WASD` or arrow keys: walk or drive
- `Shift`: run on foot
- `E`: interact, enter/exit truck, or unload at the market
- `W` / Up: accelerate
- `S` / Down: brake, then reverse
- `A` / D or Left / Right: steer the truck's heading
- `M`: island map and landmarks
- `J`: field journal, contract, inventory, and vehicle status
- `I` or `Esc`: rest house and week management
- `1`-`5`: choose an activity in a site panel
- `Space`: cast/reel while fishing
- `F5`: save
- `F9`: load

## What to Do

The salmon pens are the main farm destination, northeast of the starting farm yard. Look for four large, labeled net pens (A-D), the persistent farm-objective card, or the teal pens marker on the minimap. Walk up and press `E` to feed, maintain water, treat lice, check the hatchery batch, or load fish.

Visit the blue-marked Smolt Hatchery near the pens and press `E` to enter. Walk to the incubators, nursery, fish-health lab, and grading flume; press `E` at a station and use its number keys. Start with fertilized eggs, advance farm weeks at the rest house, feed and monitor the nursery, vaccinate transfer-ready smolts, grade them, then transfer the certified batch into the open-water pens. Hatchery stage, quality, feed, and room position are saved with the career.

Drive the loaded truck to the fish market to complete deliveries and customer contracts. Other island sites include a feed mill, town board, research station, truck workshop, coastal fishing spot, forest trail, pier, and rest house. Improve the farm and truck, gather beach finds, fish for extra income, and build reputation over an open-ended career.
